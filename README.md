# cookie-cutter
Designed in Bonn - printed anywhere!

itemis cookie cutter for 3d printing. Designed by FHU and NBU. Take a look at the gallery for pictures. For printing use the stl file. Enjoy!

# recipe
- 400g flour - Type 405
- 1 teaspoon of baking soda
- 1 pinch of salt
- 250g cold butter
- 150g sugar
- 1 Pck. Vanille sugar
- 2 eggs

Mix all ingredients by hand in a bowl to create the dough. Make two balls out of it and store it for 30min in the fridge. Pre-heat your oven to 180°C. Make your cookies and bake them approx. 8-10minutes. Enjoy!

Hint:
- Use some flour, starch, or powder sugar to make the dough less sticky while cutting
